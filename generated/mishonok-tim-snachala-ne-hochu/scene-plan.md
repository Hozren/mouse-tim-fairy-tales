# Раскадровка

Дата: 2026-11-25. Автор: Аркан / ИИ. Источник: story.md, замысел пользователя. 17 сцен и обложка.

## scene-01 — Будущий корабль

Tim in blue sweater and navy trousers kneels next to a plain large cardboard box, Teddy beside it; father holds exactly two cardboard tubes. Cozy bedroom before bedtime. Excited planning.

## scene-02 — Ещё одно окно

Tim in blue sweater compares blue and green paper circles against his cardboard box, reluctant to leave. Mother at open bedroom doorway gently invites him. No assembled engines yet.

## scene-03 — Колечко из хвоста

Tim fully clothed in blue sweater sits on striped bathroom rug looking at his curled pink tail, reluctant. Mother crouches nearby listening. Sink, toothbrush cup and hanging towel behind them. No washing yet.

## scene-04 — Морской мышь

Tim wearing blue sweater and navy trousers lies belly down on bed pretending to swim. Father sits beside bed holding pale blue two-piece pajamas with a small yellow crescent moon emblem. Kind amused faces.

## scene-05 — На полёт уже поздно

Tim now wearing pale blue pajamas with yellow crescent emblem hugs Teddy sadly by still-empty undecorated cardboard box. Father sits beside him kindly, no scolding, quiet late evening.

## scene-06 — Куда делся вечер

Close intimate composition: Tim in pale blue moon pajamas and father sitting on carpet beside unfinished plain cardboard box, talking. Tim thoughtful, father empathetic, no pointing or lecturing.

## scene-07 — Капитан подождёт

Father tucks Tim in pale blue moon pajamas into bed with affection. Teddy lies on a soft handkerchief inside still-unfinished plain cardboard box at wall. Warm bedside lamp.

## scene-08 — Три карточки

Next evening: Tim in blue sweater and father sit at a small table with exactly three separate picture cards in one row: water droplet, toothbrush, pajamas, pictograms only no lettering. Teddy sits beside cards. Two cardboard tubes nearby.

## scene-09 — Сначала вода

Tim in blue sweater stands safely on a small bathroom step stool turning on faucet and testing stream with one paw. Mother standing close to help, encouraging but not pushing. Medium view.

## scene-10 — Щётка не гонится

Tim in blue sweater brushes teeth at bathroom sink; father is close by gently guiding the toothbrush hand, no force. Three cards on dry shelf: first face down, second toothbrush pictogram visible, third pajamas pictogram visible. Toothpaste and cup.

## scene-11 — Заблудившееся ухо

Tim already wearing pale blue pajama trousers is pulling matching pajama top over his head, one big ear caught briefly in neck opening. Father gently loosens fabric to help. Comic friendly moment, no distress or impossible limbs. Bed in background.

## scene-12 — Корабль готов

Tim in pale blue moon pajamas carefully seats Teddy inside cardboard spaceship: one blue circular paper window outside and exactly two cardboard tubes attached as engines. Father sits on rug beside box, helping. Joyful warm bedroom.

## scene-13 — Лунная пещера

Tim in pale blue moon pajamas moves Teddy nose-first into one of father’s slippers on carpet, beside two pillows that form lunar rocks. Finished cardboard ship with blue round window and two tubes nearby; father smiles sitting on carpet. Imaginary moon remains domestic play, no real space.

## scene-14 — Одну лапку, другую

Morning bedroom, sunlight: sleepy Tim in pale blue moon pajamas sitting on mother’s lap at edge of bed, lowering his feet toward floor after a cuddle. Mother wears cream blouse and green skirt. Gentle start of day.

## scene-15 — Можно перевести дух

After preschool: tired Tim sitting leaning against mother on entryway bench. Tim still in zipped blue outdoor coat, navy trousers and brown boots. Mother beside him, her bag on floor. Completed cardboard ship visible through open doorway. Both resting quietly.

## scene-16 — Лапки готовы

Tim now in dry indoor blue sweater and navy trousers at bathroom sink, standing on step stool, rubbing soap foam between paws under gentle stream of water. Towel beside him. Alone in frame, focused and calm. No outdoor coat or boots.

## scene-17 — Луна подождёт

Tim in blue sweater and navy trousers lies relaxed on back on bedroom rug with Teddy on his belly; mother sits on carpet holding his free paw. Completed cardboard spaceship with one blue window and two tube engines beside them. Peaceful afternoon, no demand to play.
